 creative audio software developer

I'm a Berlin-based software engineer and music technologist. I've been building music software since 2003, across academia and industry, and I'm a prolific open-source contributor — maintaining audio frameworks and tools used by developers around the world. Along the way I've collaborated with well-known brands and celebrated sound artists.
